State of the Game

Hello, world.

My name is Alex. I'm a systems analyst and an oil painter from Brazil, and for the last couple of months I've been spending my free time going back to the roots of the games I love: MMOs.

So I started building my own, and now I want to see what other people feel when they play it.

It's an alpha, so things will break.

— Alex
